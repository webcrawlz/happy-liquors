Terms of Use

Last updated: 2 October 2026

1. Who we are

Happy Liquors, 29 Bharathiyar Road, near Old Bus Stand, Kilinjalmedu, Karaikal, Puducherry 609602, is a licensed liquor retail vendor operating under the Puducherry Excise Act, 1970 and the rules framed thereunder. This website is our digital shelf — a catalogue of what we stock, with tools to help you choose and to send us an order request on WhatsApp.

2. 18+ only

Under Section 196(C) of the Puducherry Excise Act, 1970, the sale or supply of liquor to any person below the age of 18 is a punishable offence. You must be 18 years or older to use this website or to purchase from us. We may ask for a valid photo ID at the store, and we will refuse sale where the law requires it.

3. What this website does

Everything here — the collection, the bottle finder, party packs, offers and the Pour Journal — is for information and order requests only. Nothing on this site is a confirmed sale until the shop confirms it.

4. Ordering on WhatsApp

Tapping “Order on WhatsApp” composes a chat message to our store number (+91 73737 33998) containing your cart and, if you entered it, your mobile number. This is a request, not an order confirmation. The shop will confirm availability, the final price and collection details on WhatsApp. The sale is completed only at our licensed premises during permitted trading hours.

5. Prices & availability

Prices shown here are indicative and follow the prevailing excise-approved rates; they may change without notice. Availability is subject to stock at the store. If something in your request is unavailable, we will suggest an alternative on WhatsApp.

6. Dry days & trading hours

We remain closed on dry days notified by the Excise Department — including Gandhi Jayanthi and any day specified by the Deputy Commissioner under Rule 199-A of the Puducherry Excise Rules, 1970, as well as election-related closures. Order requests received on dry days will be taken up on the next working day.

7. Right to refuse sale

In line with the excise rules, we will not sell liquor to persons below 18 years of age, to persons who appear to be intoxicated, or in any other circumstance where the law prohibits the sale. Our team’s decision at the counter is final.

8. Offers & store credit

Promotional offers, including the review reward of up to ₹100 in store credit, are at the shop’s discretion and redeemable only on a future visit, on showing the published review at the counter. Google may remove reviews under its own policies; we cannot control or guarantee that a review stays published.

9. Drink responsibly

Alcohol is injurious to health. Never drink and drive. If you are pregnant, please do not consume alcohol. Nothing on this site encourages excessive consumption.

12. Changes & contact

We may update these terms from time to time; the version on this page applies. Questions? Reach us on WhatsApp at +91 73737 33998 or visit the store.
