Your data, honestly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

1. Our approach: we collect almost nothing

Happy Liquors runs this website without user accounts, without a customer database and without analytics or advertising trackers. There is no server on our side storing your browsing. What follows is the complete picture of what happens with your information.

2. What stays on your own device

Your browser keeps a few things locally so the site works smoothly:

  • Your cart — the bottles you add, saved in your browser’s local storage.
  • Your mobile number (optional) — only if you type it into the cart; it stays in your browser until you remove it.
  • Your language choice — English or Tamil, remembered for your next visit.
  • Age confirmation — a flag for the current browsing session so you aren’t asked twice.

All of this lives on your device, not with us. Clearing your browser’s site data erases it permanently.

3. What you share when you order

When you tap “Order on WhatsApp”, your browser opens a WhatsApp chat with our store number (+91 73737 33998) containing your cart items and — only if you entered it — your mobile number. From that moment, that message is a WhatsApp conversation between you and the shop, governed by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy. We use it solely to confirm and fulfil your order request.

4. What we don’t do

  • No analytics, cookies for tracking, or advertising pixels.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information with third parties for marketing.
  • No profiling. We don’t build customer profiles — we run a shop, not a data business.

5. Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to know what personal data is held about you and to request correction or erasure. Since we hold no customer database, the practical answer is simple: anything the site remembers about you is in your own browser (see section 2), and you can erase it at any time by clearing site data. If you’ve shared your number with us on WhatsApp and want it deleted from our chat records, message us on +91 73737 33998 and we will delete it.

6. Children

This website and our store serve adults aged 18 and above only, in line with the Puducherry Excise Act, 1970. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

7. Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we’ll update this page and the “last updated” date above. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact & grievance

For any privacy question, correction or deletion request, reach us on WhatsApp at +91 73737 33998 or visit us at 29 Bharathiyar Road, Karaikal. We will respond within a reasonable time.

This policy describes our actual practices in plain language. It is not legal advice — for anything beyond the ordinary, please consult a lawyer.
